# Cohort-Definition Checklist

- Confirm the target concept identifier and its intended clinical meaning from an approved vocabulary source.
- Record the index-date rule, minimum observation requirements, age restriction, exclusions, and censoring rules.
- Report missing identifiers, duplicate records, invalid dates, and references to unknown persons.
- Keep row-level data inside the approved project boundary.
- Treat aggregate-count suppression as one control rather than export authorization.
